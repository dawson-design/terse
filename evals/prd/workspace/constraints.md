# Constraints

- Notifications go through the department's Notify service. It sends SMS and
  email from pre-approved templates. A new template takes two weeks of
  content review.
- No personal health information in a text or an email. The message can name
  the facility and the new status, and must link to the portal for details.
- SMS needs the veteran's opt-in (TCPA). Email can go to the address on file
  unless the veteran has opted out.
- Templates and the portal pages must meet Section 508 and the plain-language
  content standards.
- Pilot: two facilities, Tucson and Boise, for 8 weeks starting 2027-02-01.
  National rollout depends on pilot results.
