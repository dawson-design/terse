# Discovery: appointment request status

Owner: Marcus Lee (product). Research: Aisha Bello (design). October 2026.

## What we heard (6 veteran interviews, 2 call-center shadowing sessions)

- "I checked the portal every day for two weeks. Nothing changed, then the
  clinic called me." (interview 2)
- "I didn't know they needed more information until it was too late and they
  closed my request." (interview 5)
- Four of six veterans said they call the call center to find out where a
  request stands. Two said they gave up and went to urgent care.
- Call-center agents look up the request status and read it out. Agents said
  this is most of their "routine" calls.

## Numbers

- Q3 2026 call-center data: 108,400 calls, of which 41,200 (38%) were tagged
  "where's my request".
- Requests move submitted -> in review -> scheduled, needs more information, or
  declined. "Needs more information" requests close after 14 days with no
  reply. 22% of those closed with no reply in Q3.

## Ideas we discussed

- Text and email when the status changes. Happy path is submitted ->
  scheduled.
- Rescheduling by replying to the text (parked: the scheduling system can't
  take it).
- Push notifications in the mobile app (parked: the app team's roadmap is
  full until summer).

## Open

- Marcus wants "a meaningful drop" in where's-my-request calls. No target
  number agreed yet.
- Who signs off on the notification templates? Probably the content team,
  not confirmed.
